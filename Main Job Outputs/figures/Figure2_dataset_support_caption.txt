Class support for the paper evaluation sets. Tufts: all 1000 usable cases × 32 Universal slots (the earlier 100-case runs are archive only). DENTEX: QED train+val minus 3 held-out train text-exemplars (752 images). The first two DENTEX bars are mutually exclusive 32-slot counts: unique abnormal teeth 3,627 + no-finding slots 20,437 = 24,064. Disease bars are GT-positive tooth-slot support (n) under one-vs-rest scoring: Impacted 642, Caries 2286, Deep Caries 602, Periapical Lesion 166 (sum 3696). These four n do not sum to unique abnormal teeth because a multi-label tooth is counted in every disease it carries. 2290/610 were unofficial sums of the DENTEX paper's train totals plus val; canonical unique (image, FDI) on-disk QED before held-out exclusion is Caries 2287 and Deep Caries 607.
